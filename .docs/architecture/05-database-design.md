# 05. Database Design

## Main Tables

- Users
- Residents
- Rooms
- Visitors
- Bills
- Maintenance Requests
- Face Templates
- OCR Records

## Notes

- Users store authentication and profile information.
- Residents represent people living in the dormitory.
- Rooms represent available accommodation units.
- Visitors represent external people who visit the dormitory.
- Bills store charging and payment-related information.
- Maintenance Requests track facility repair requests.
- Face Templates store biometric enrollment data.
- OCR Records store extracted information from documents.
